About Pricealerter

Pricealerter is an independent information website designed to help readers explore the Indian primary market. The site brings IPO information together in one place so visitors can review issue schedules and compare publicly available issue details.

What you can explore

  • Upcoming, open, closed and recently listed IPOs.
  • Issue details such as price band, lot size, issue size and important dates, where available.
  • Subscription figures and category-wise data, where available.
  • Grey Market Premium (GMP) indicators and historical snapshots, where available.
  • IPO calendars, listing information and educational calculators.

Our approach

We aim to present information clearly and distinguish reported figures from estimates or unofficial indicators. Data may be delayed, revised or unavailable. Readers should verify important details with the issuer's offer documents, stock exchange notices, registrar and other official sources before making decisions.

Editorial and financial independence

Pricealerter is an informational resource, not a stockbroker, investment adviser, merchant banker or issuer. We do not accept IPO applications or provide personalized investment recommendations.
