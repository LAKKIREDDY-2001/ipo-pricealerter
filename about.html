Our Mission

Navigating the primary markets in India can be overwhelming, with critical information scattered across stock exchanges, merchant banker disclosures, registrar databases, and grey market forums. Our mission is to democratize IPO research by providing retail investors with lightning-fast, structured, and unbiased intelligence in one unified dashboard.

What We Provide

  • Live & Historical IPO Data: Real-time bidding status, lot sizes, price bands, and issue size breakdowns for both NSE and BSE.
  • Grey Market Premium (GMP) Tracking: Daily estimated trend updates, calculated expected listing gains, and risk assessments.
  • Subscription Analytics: Consolidated subscription multiples across QIB, NII/HNI, and Retail investor categories.
  • IPO Calendar: Comprehensive day-by-day roadmap detailing IPO opening dates, bidding closures, basis of allotment, demat credit, and exchange listings.
  • Official Allotment Checkers: One-click direct links to verified registrar allotment query portals (Bigshare, KFintech, Link Intime, Maashitla, MUFG Link/MPMS, Purva, Cameo, Skyline, etc.).
  • Knowledge Center: Beginner-friendly guides, application walkthroughs (e.g., Groww, Zerodha UPI ASBA), and answers to frequently asked questions.

Data Integrity & Objectivity

We believe in absolute transparency. We aggregate factual data directly from official exchange notices (NSE/BSE), SEBI prospectuses (DRHP/RHP), and verified registrars. We are not affiliated with any stockbroker, investment bank, or company issuing an IPO.

Monetization & Free Access

To ensure that all analytics remain 100% free and accessible to all retail investors without subscription paywalls, this platform is supported through Google AdSense advertising.
