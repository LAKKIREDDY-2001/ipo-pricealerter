About IPO Pricealerter

Democratizing Primary Market Intelligence for Indian Retail Investors

IPO Pricealerter (accessible at https://pricealerter.in/) is an independent financial data tracking portal designed to provide Indian retail investors with real-time, transparent, and verified intelligence on Initial Public Offerings across both Mainboard and SME segments.

Our Purpose

PriceAlerter is an independently operated website that brings Indian IPO information and listed-share reference data into one place. It is designed to make issue schedules, offer terms, allotment links, listing records, market-price references and educational explanations easier for retail readers to navigate.

We aim to distinguish official company and exchange information from third-party estimates. Grey Market Premium (GMP) is unofficial and speculative; it is not an exchange quotation or a reliable promise of listing performance.

How we handle information

  • Official documents first: For offer terms, dates and company disclosures, readers should rely on issuer offer documents and official NSE/BSE announcements. PriceAlerter summaries are for convenience and should be checked against those originals.
  • Market prices: Listed-share quotes can be delayed, unavailable or dependent on third-party data providers. Check the displayed source and timestamp; verify important decisions with your broker or exchange.
  • Historical records: Listing-day prices and previous closes are historical values and must not be confused with the current market price.
  • GMP: We label GMP as an unofficial market indication. It can be incomplete, unverified or stale and should never be treated as guaranteed profit.
  • Corrections: Readers can report an error with the page URL and an official supporting source using our Contact page.

Who operates the website

PriceAlerter is maintained by its independent site operator. It is not a stock exchange, broker, registrar, merchant banker, investment adviser or SEBI-registered research analyst. We do not claim to operate direct exchange order-book or registrar integrations unless a specific feature clearly identifies its provider and update status.

Editorial responsibility

We seek to publish original educational explanations, identify important source limitations, correct errors when reliable evidence is provided and clearly distinguish advertising from editorial material. Read our Editorial Standards & Data Methodology for more detail.

Technology Stack & Privacy Commitment

PriceAlerter is built on a high-speed, lightweight static-first architecture optimized for swift loading on mobile networks across India. We believe in strict data privacy:

  • We never ask for your Demat login credentials, trading passwords, or bank account numbers.
  • All allotment queries direct you securely to the official SSL-encrypted portals of the designated registrars.
  • Our educational content and dashboards are 100% free to access, supported by non-intrusive standard web advertising from Google AdSense.

Regulatory & Legal Stance

IPO Pricealerter operates strictly as an educational and information aggregation portal. We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). All content published on this website is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Always consult a SEBI-registered professional before making investment decisions.
