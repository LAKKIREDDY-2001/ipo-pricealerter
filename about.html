Our Core Mission

For decades, the Indian primary market suffered from severe information asymmetry. While institutional investors had immediate access to Bloomberg terminals, private merchant banker roadshows, and syndicated research, retail investors were left navigating fragmented exchange filings, delayed registrar queries, or unregulated WhatsApp tips.

Our mission is simple: to equalize access to primary market data. We aggregate, standardize, and present complex equity market disclosures—from Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filings to real-time subscription multiples and registrar allotment servers—into an intuitive, mobile-first analytics platform accessible to every citizen investor.

What Sets IPO Pricealerter Apart?

Our Guiding Operating Principles

  • Zero Paid Promotions: We do not accept sponsored placements from issuers or merchant bankers to give favorable reviews to any IPO.
  • No "Buy/Sell" Tips: We firmly believe retail investors should not rely on anonymous stock tips. We present factual data, financial statements, and valuation multiples so you can make informed decisions.
  • Real-Time Data Feeds: Direct integrations and automated checks with NSE, BSE, Link Intime, and KFintech ensure you see live subscription tallies and allotment links without delay.
  • Transparent Grey Market Disclosures: Unlike blogs that present GMP as guaranteed profits, we prominently educate investors about the unofficial, unregulated nature of the grey market.

Our Editorial & Verification Methodology

Every IPO listed on PriceAlerter undergoes a rigorous 4-step data verification lifecycle:

  1. Official DRHP & RHP Review: Our research team reviews the statutory Draft Red Herring Prospectus filed with SEBI, extracting the exact fresh issue size, offer for sale (OFS) components, price band, and 3-year restated financial statements.
  2. Exchange Circular Cross-Referencing: Dates, lot sizes, cut-off prices, and Anchor Investor allocations are validated against official exchange circulars issued by BSE and NSE.
  3. Live Bidding Synchronization: During the 3-day public bidding window, subscription figures across Retail (RII), Non-Institutional (NII/HNI), and Qualified Institutional Buyer (QIB) categories are tracked and refreshed directly against exchange order books.
  4. Registrar Basis of Allotment Direct Links: As soon as the appointed Registrar (Link Intime, KFintech, Bigshare, etc.) finalizes the basis of allotment, direct query shortcuts are published to help users verify their status instantly without navigation friction.

Technology Stack & Privacy Commitment

PriceAlerter is built on a high-speed, lightweight static-first architecture optimized for swift loading on mobile networks across India. We believe in strict data privacy:

  • We never ask for your Demat login credentials, trading passwords, or bank account numbers.
  • All allotment queries direct you securely to the official SSL-encrypted portals of the designated registrars.
  • Our educational content and dashboards are 100% free to access, supported by non-intrusive standard web advertising from Google AdSense.

Regulatory & Legal Stance

IPO Pricealerter operates strictly as an educational and information aggregation portal. We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA). All content published on this website is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice. Always consult a SEBI-registered professional before making investment decisions.
