Editorial Standards & Data Methodology

Last updated: 6 November 2026

PriceAlerter is an independent information platform covering Indian IPOs, issue schedules, subscription data, unofficial grey-market indicators and listed-share information. This page explains how information is selected, presented and corrected. It is intended to help readers understand both the usefulness and the limitations of our data.

Important: PriceAlerter is an informational and educational website. It is not a stock exchange, broker, investment adviser, research analyst or registrar. Nothing on this website is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold a security.

1. Our content and editorial approach

We aim to publish useful, clearly written explanations of IPO processes and structured market information. Educational articles should explain concepts in our own words, provide context and distinguish facts from estimates or opinion. We do not intentionally republish complete articles, paid research, prospectuses or other copyrighted works. Where third-party information is used, we identify the source or link to the original publisher where practical.

Our coverage may include issue terms, price bands, lot sizes, dates, registrar links, subscription figures, listing prices, closing prices and listed-share quotes. Data availability differs by field and provider.

2. Sources and verification

  • Official issue information: We prioritise company offer documents, exchange notices and issuer announcements, including sources published by NSE and BSE.
  • Allotment: Allotment status is determined by the appointed registrar and/or official exchange facility. We link users to the relevant official service where available.
  • Market prices: Prices may be supplied by third-party market-data services or exchange-distributed data. A displayed quote should be read with its source, timestamp and market-session status where provided.
  • GMP: Grey Market Premium is an unofficial, opaque and unregulated market indication. It is not an exchange price, may be based on incomplete reports and can change quickly. We do not present GMP as verified exchange data or a guaranteed listing outcome.

We cannot independently audit every third-party feed or guarantee that every value is complete, real-time or error-free. If a value is missing, delayed or not verified, it should be labelled accordingly rather than inferred or fabricated.

3. Updates, timestamps and historical data

Market data can change during trading hours and may be delayed by the upstream provider. A timestamp indicates the latest update known to the website, not a guarantee of exchange-level real-time delivery. Historical listing and closing prices are presented as records and should not be confused with the current market price. Corrections may be made when a source is updated or an error is identified.

4. Corrections and reader feedback

We welcome specific reports about inaccurate dates, prices, company details, broken links or unclear explanations. Please include the page URL, the field in question and a reliable supporting source, preferably an official exchange, issuer or registrar document. We review submissions and update content when the evidence supports a correction. Sending a report does not guarantee that it will be published or that a change will be made.

5. User submissions and moderation

Where the website accepts comments, feedback, alert requests or other user-submitted material, submissions may be reviewed, filtered, limited or removed. Do not submit personal financial credentials, passwords, full payment details, PAN, Aadhaar or other sensitive identity information. We do not allow spam, impersonation, abusive content, unlawful material, misleading investment claims or content that infringes another person's rights. User submissions do not represent the views of PriceAlerter.

6. Independence and advertising

PriceAlerter may display advertisements, including ads served by Google. Advertising helps support the website. Advertisements are separate from editorial content; paid placements, if introduced, should be clearly identified. The presence of an advertisement does not mean that PriceAlerter endorses the advertiser, product or service. We do not promise that an advertisement or external link is suitable for every visitor.

7. Financial risk and external links

Investing in shares and IPOs involves risk, including loss of capital. GMP is speculative and should not be used as the sole basis for an investment decision. Verify offer details, dates, eligibility and market prices with official sources before acting. External websites are operated by third parties and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
