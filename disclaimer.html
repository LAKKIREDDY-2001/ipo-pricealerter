2. Grey Market Premium (GMP) Notice & Risk Disclosure

IPO Pricealerter displays Grey Market Premium (GMP) data, Kostak rates, and Subject to Sauda indicators. Users must clearly understand the fundamental nature of the grey market:

  • Informal & Unregulated: The grey market operates strictly outside the legal oversight and regulatory jurisdiction of SEBI, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the National Stock Exchange (NSE), and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). Contracts executed in the grey market have zero statutory backing, settlement guarantee, or investor grievance mechanisms.
  • Sentiment Indicator, Not Valuation: GMP is an informal sentiment gauge derived from localized market inquiries and unofficial dealer books. It does not represent fundamental enterprise valuation or guaranteed secondary market clearing prices.
  • High Reversal Risk: GMP figures can collapse within minutes due to shifts in broader market volatility, institutional subscription updates, or changes in regulatory policy. An IPO exhibiting a high GMP during the bidding window may list at a discount or flat on listing morning.
  • No Solicitation: IPO Pricealerter does not facilitate, broker, trade, or clear grey market contracts or secondary market trades. GMP data is cataloged solely as a market commentary metric.

4. Inherent Market Risks in Equity Offerings

Investing in public equity offerings entails substantial market risks:

  • Listing Day Price Volatility: Newly listed shares frequently experience extreme intraday volatility driven by anchor investor lock-ins, institutional rebalancing, and speculative momentum.
  • Business & Macroeconomic Risks: Companies raising capital may face industry downturns, operational bottlenecks, debt servicing stress, raw material price inflation, or management turnover as disclosed in the "Risk Factors" chapter of their respective prospectuses.
  • No Guaranteed Listing Gains: Past listing performance across historical IPOs bears zero correlation to future offerings. Investors can and do lose capital on primary market subscriptions.

5. SME IPO Platform Special Risk Notice

Offerings listed on SME platforms (BSE SME and NSE Emerge) carry elevated risks compared to mainboard listings:

  • Liquidity & Block Size Trading: SME shares trade strictly in pre-defined lot sizes (typically 1,000 to 4,000 shares per lot), requiring minimum capital commitments of ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000. Selling fractional quantities is prohibited by exchange regulations.
  • Wide Bid-Ask Spreads: In periods of market stress, market makers may widen spreads, causing significant price slippage upon exit.
  • Surveillance Frameworks: Micro-cap equities are frequently subjected to Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) and daily circuit filters of 5% or 10% by exchanges to curb erratic volatility.
