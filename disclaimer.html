2. Grey Market Premium (GMP) Notice

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is an unofficial, unregulated over-the-counter (OTC) indication traded informally before stock listing. It is not approved, verified, monitored, or endorsed by stock exchanges (NSE / BSE) or regulatory authorities (SEBI). GMP numbers are volatile, based on market sentiment, and subject to swift reversals. A positive GMP does not ensure listing day gains or price stability. Never make investment choices based solely on GMP figures.

3. Data Accuracy & Third-Party Verification

IPO Pricealerter aggregates public information from official exchange portals (NSE India, BSE India), market news aggregators (Chittorgarh, IPO Watch), registrar portals (Link Intime, KFin Technologies, Bigshare Services, Maashitla, MUFG Link/MPMS, Purva Share, Skyline, Mas Services), and Red Herring Prospectuses (RHP). While we strive for absolute accuracy and update stats frequently, figures may experience delays, revisions, or technical errors. Users are strongly advised to verify details with official offer documents (DRHP / RHP) filed with SEBI.

4. Investment Risk Warning

Equity investments, especially in Initial Public Offerings (both Mainboard and SME segments), carry significant risk, including partial or total capital loss. SME IPOs typically have minimum lot requirements of ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 and lower liquidity compared to Mainboard equities. Investors are solely responsible for their financial decisions.
