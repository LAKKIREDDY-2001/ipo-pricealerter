Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently are IPO Grey Market Premiums (GMP) updated?

GMP is unofficial and may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. We do not guarantee a fixed update schedule. Check any displayed source and timestamp, and verify important information independently.

Can IPO Pricealerter guarantee IPO allotment?

No. IPO allotment in India is conducted electronically by the appointed registrar (such as Link Intime or KFintech) via a randomized, computerized lottery supervised by stock exchanges. No external entity can influence or guarantee allotment.

How do I report a factual error in an IPO summary?

Please email us at lakkistudy@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Correction: [Company Name]" and include the relevant section of the official DRHP or exchange circular for rapid verification.
