Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently are IPO Grey Market Premiums (GMP) updated?

Our research desk updates GMP estimates twice daily—once after the morning dealer updates (around 10:30 AM IST) and once post market close (around 6:00 PM IST) to capture sentiment changes accurately.

Can IPO Pricealerter guarantee IPO allotment?

No. IPO allotment in India is conducted electronically by the appointed registrar (such as Link Intime or KFintech) via a randomized, computerized lottery supervised by stock exchanges. No external entity can influence or guarantee allotment.

How do I report a factual error in an IPO summary?

Please email us at contact@pricealerter.in with the subject line "Data Correction: [Company Name]" and include the relevant section of the official DRHP or exchange circular for rapid verification.
