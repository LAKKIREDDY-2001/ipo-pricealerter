2. Log Files & Analytics

Like many web sites, IPO Pricealerter follows a standard procedure of using log files. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Our interactive features, including investor community comments and search preferences, store data locally in your browser's localStorage. No financial credentials, passwords, or Demat account numbers are ever collected, accessed, or stored by IPO Pricealerter.
