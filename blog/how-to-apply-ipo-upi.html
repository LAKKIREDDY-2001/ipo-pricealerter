How to Apply for an IPO in India — Step by Step (UPI & ASBA Guide)

By Venkata Madhava Reddy (Lead Financial Analyst) • 13 min read • Published: October 2026 • Updated: November 3, 2026

Applying for an Initial Public Offering in India was once an agonizing, paper-intensive exercise requiring multi-page application forms, physical signatures, bank demand drafts, and weeks of postal delays. Today, through the visionary integration of ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount) and UPI 2.0 (Unified Payments Interface) mandated by SEBI, any retail investor can research, submit, and confirm an IPO bid in less than 90 seconds directly from their smartphone.

However, despite the seamless technology, tens of thousands of retail IPO bids are rejected by exchange order books every single month due to preventable mistakes: mismatched PAN details, incorrect UPI handles, bidding below the cut-off price, or failing to approve mandates before the strict 5:00 PM cutoff. In this guide, we provide an exhaustive, zero-error blueprint for applying through top broker apps and Net Banking portals.

The Golden Rule: The Identical PAN Mandate

Under SEBI regulations, the Permanent Account Number (PAN) associated with your Demat account, your bank account, and your UPI payment application must match identically. Applying from a family member's UPI handle or a corporate bank account for a personal Demat account triggers automated disqualification during registrar clearing.

Prerequisites for Submitting an IPO Application

Before initiating a bid, ensure the following three foundational accounts are active and properly linked:

  • Demat & Trading Account: Held with any SEBI-registered Depository Participant (DP) registered under NSDL or CDSL (e.g., Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox, ICICI Direct).
  • Bank Account with ASBA Capability: With sufficient unencumbered funds to cover the minimum lot investment value (typically ₹14,000–₹15,000 for Mainboard; ₹1,00,000–₹2,40,000 for SME issues).
  • UPI 2.0 Enabled Application: Such as Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM, Paytm, or your bank's native mobile banking application.

Method 1: Applying via Stock Broker App with UPI ASBA

Follow these five streamlined steps when bidding through mobile broker apps during active market hours (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST on open bidding days):

Step 1: Navigate to the IPO Bids Section

Open your broker app (Zerodha Kite, Groww, Angel One, Upstox). Tap on the Bids / IPO section from the main navigation menu to view active public issues.

Step 2: Select the Company and Specify Lot Count

Select the active company you want to bid for and click Apply. Enter your desired quantity in multiples of the prescribed Lot Size. For instance, if 1 lot = 38 shares, bidding for 2 lots auto-populates 76 shares.

Step 3: Always Select the "Cut-Off Price" Checkbox

Always check the Cut-Off Price box. This ensures your bid dynamically matches the final discovered price decided by the book-running lead managers, guaranteeing your application stays eligible for the computerized allotment lottery.

Step 4: Enter Your Verified UPI ID

Enter your personal UPI VPA (e.g., yourname@okhdfcbank, yourname@ybl). Confirm the total investment amount and hit Submit Bid. The broker immediately transmits your electronic order to the exchange order book.

Step 5: Authorize the UPI Mandate Before 5:00 PM

Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM). You will receive an incoming mandate request notification from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). Verify the amount, enter your secret UPI PIN, and approve the block. The funds remain safely in your bank account, continuing to earn savings interest.

Method 2: Applying via Net Banking ASBA (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis)

For high-value applications exceeding ₹5 Lakhs, High Net-worth Individual (HNI) quotas, or investors who prefer not to use UPI, Net Banking ASBA is the institutional gold standard:

  1. Log into your Net Banking portal (e.g., HDFC NetBanking, SBI Online, ICICI Bank).
  2. Navigate to Investments & Insurance → e-Services → ASBA / IPO Application.
  3. Select the active IPO from the list and choose your investor category (Retail or HNI).
  4. Choose your Depository (NSDL or CDSL), and enter your 16-digit Demat number (DP ID + Client ID).
  5. Enter your lot quantity, check Cut-off price, and confirm. Your bank directly places a lien on the required balance instantly with zero UPI delay.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the deadline for approving the UPI mandate on closing day?

Under current SEBI regulations, the bidding window closes on exchange order books at 5:00 PM IST on the final closing day. While exchanges accept bids until 5:00 PM, investors must approve their UPI mandate request promptly. It is highly recommended to authorize mandates before 4:30 PM to avoid network congestion.

Can I apply for an IPO using a family member's UPI ID?

No. SEBI strictly disallows third-party UPI applications. The PAN registered with your stock broker (Demat account) must identically match the PAN of the bank account linked to the UPI ID. Mismatched applications are automatically rejected during registrar clearing.

Is my money immediately debited when I approve the UPI mandate?

No. Your money is only blocked (placed under lien) in your existing bank account. It never leaves your bank during bidding and continues earning regular savings account interest. The funds are only debited on T+2 day if you are successfully allotted shares.
