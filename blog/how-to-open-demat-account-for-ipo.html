How to Open a Demat Account for IPO Applications in India

By Venkata Madhava Reddy (Lead Financial Analyst) • 12 min read • Published: October 2026 • Updated: November 3, 2026

To bid for any Initial Public Offering in India, holding an active Demat (Dematerialized) Account is mandatory under SEBI regulations. A Demat account holds your equity shares in secure electronic format, eliminating the risks of physical share certificate theft or loss.

In this guide, we walk you through the seamless paperless e-KYC procedure for opening a Demat account in less than 10 minutes from your smartphone.

CDSL vs. NSDL: Understanding the Depositories

India has two national depositories regulated by SEBI: Central Depository Services Limited (CDSL) and National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL). Both depositories operate with state-of-the-art cryptographic security. Your broker automatically assigns your Demat account to either CDSL (16-digit numeric BOID) or NSDL (starts with 'IN' followed by 14 digits).

Documents Required for Instant e-KYC

  • PAN Card: Your physical Permanent Account Number card.
  • Aadhaar Card: Linked to your active mobile number for instant OTP verification via Digilocker.
  • Bank Account Details: Bank statement or cancelled cheque clearly displaying your name, account number, and IFSC code.
  • Digital Signature: A clear photo of your signature on plain white paper.
  • Video In-Person Verification (IPV): A 5-second selfie video recorded via your mobile camera to verify identity.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does it take to activate a Demat account for IPO bidding?

With paperless Digilocker and Aadhaar OTP e-KYC, modern brokers like Zerodha, Groww, and Angel One activate your Demat account within 4 to 24 hours on business days.

Is it safe to link my bank account to a discount broker app?

Yes. Indian stock brokers are strictly regulated by SEBI. When applying for IPOs, funds are not deposited into your broker account; they remain safely blocked inside your personal bank account via the ASBA framework.

Can a minor open a Demat account to apply for IPOs?

Yes. A minor can open a Demat account operated by their legal natural guardian. However, minor accounts cannot execute intraday trading and must apply for IPOs via Net Banking ASBA.
