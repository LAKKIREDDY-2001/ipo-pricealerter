How to Open a Demat Account for IPO Applications in India

By IPO Pricealerter Research Team Published: 07 November 2024 Updated: 15 November 2024 Reading Time: 8 mins

To bid for shares in an Initial Public Offering in India, holding an active Demat (Dematerialized) Account is a non-negotiable statutory requirement mandated by SEBI. In modern capital markets, physical share certificates have been completely abolished; every equity share allotted to an investor is credited electronically into a central depository account.

Thanks to India's digital public infrastructure (Aadhaar e-KYC and DigiLocker), opening a SEBI-registered Demat and Trading account takes less than 10 minutes completely online. Here is your definitive guide to getting IPO-ready.

Understanding the Tripartite Account Structure

When entering the stock market, you interact with three interconnected accounts:

  • Bank Account: Holds your liquid cash. Under the ASBA mechanism, your funds remain securely in your own bank account earning interest until allotment is finalized.
  • Trading Account: Managed by your stockbroker (e.g., Zerodha, Groww, Angel One). It serves as the bidding interface that communicates your order to the BSE/NSE exchanges.
  • Demat Account: Your electronic vault held with one of India's two national central depositories—CDSL (Central Depository Services Ltd) or NSDL (National Securities Depository Ltd). This is where your shares physically reside once allotted.

Mandatory Documents & Prerequisites

Before beginning the online registration flow, keep the following items ready:

  1. Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card: Mandatory for tax identification and SEBI cross-matching.
  2. Aadhaar Card: Must be linked to an active mobile phone number to receive the DigiLocker and NSDL electronic signature OTPs.
  3. Bank Account Details: Bank account number and IFSC code, verified via a cancelled cheque, bank statement, or penny-drop instant verification.
  4. Digital Signature: A clear photo of your signature signed on plain white paper.
  5. Smartphone / Laptop Webcam: For the 10-second In-Person Verification (IPV) selfie video.

Step-by-Step e-KYC Onboarding Process

Follow these simple steps on any SEBI-registered depository participant portal:

Step 1: Mobile & Email Verification

Download your chosen broker app or open their website. Enter your 10-digit mobile number and primary email ID. Confirm both with one-time passwords (OTPs).

Step 2: Enter PAN & Date of Birth

Input your PAN. The broker's backend performs an instant query against the Income Tax Department / NSDL database to confirm your legal name.

Step 3: Aadhaar e-KYC via DigiLocker

You will be redirected to the Government-backed DigiLocker gateway. Authorize DigiLocker to share your officially verified Aadhaar XML file. An OTP will be delivered to the mobile number registered with UIDAI.

Step 4: Bank Account Linking

Provide your savings bank account number and IFSC. Most brokers perform an automated "penny-drop" (depositing ₹1) to confirm that the bank account name matches your PAN name exactly.

⚠️ Critical Allotment Warning: The Third-Party Name Rule

SEBI strictly rejects any IPO application where the bank account holder's name or UPI handle differs from the PAN registered on the Demat account. Never attempt to apply for an IPO from a friend's Demat account using your personal bank UPI handle. Both names must be identical.

Step 5: In-Person Verification (IPV) Selfie

Take a clear photo using your front camera or record a 5-second video reciting a 4-digit verification code displayed on screen. Ensure good lighting and remove spectacles or caps.

Step 6: Digital Aadhaar e-Sign

Review the generated Demat account opening form (PDF). Click "e-Sign via Aadhaar", which redirects you to the official NSDL e-Sign portal. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number, accept the consent statement, and enter the final OTP.

Once completed, the broker typically activates your Demat account within 4 to 24 hours. You will receive your 16-digit Demat Account Number (BO ID) via email from CDSL or NSDL.

Setting Up UPI for IPO Applications

Most retail investors apply through broker apps using UPI ASBA. Ensure your UPI app supports mandate approvals up to ₹5,00,000 (the standard SEBI threshold):

  • Supported Apps: BHIM UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm UPI, CRED, or individual mobile banking apps (iMobile, YONO, Kotak 811).
  • Link the Same Bank Account: Ensure the bank account linked to your UPI handle is the exact account verified during your Demat account opening.
