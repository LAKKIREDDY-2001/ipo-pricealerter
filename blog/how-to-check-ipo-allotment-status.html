How to Check IPO Allotment Status Online — All Registrars & BSE/NSE Portals

By PriceAlerter Editorial Desk • 12 min read • Published: October 2026 • Updated: November 3, 2026

Once an IPO bidding window officially closes, investors eagerly anticipate the allotment declaration on T+1 day. Checking your allotment status online requires knowing which official registrar was appointed by the company and using verified exchange inquiry portals.

In this guide, we provide step-by-step instructions for checking your allotment status across all major Indian registrars and exchange verification portals using your Permanent Account Number (PAN).

Method 1: Checking on the Official Registrar Portal

The registrar is the authoritative institution responsible for processing all bids, validating payment mandates, and publishing the Basis of Allotment. Follow these steps for your respective registrar:

1. Link Intime India Pvt Ltd

  1. Visit the Link Intime Public Issues portal: https://linkintime.co.in/initial_offer/public-issues.html
  2. Select the company name from the dropdown list.
  3. Select PAN as your query identifier.
  4. Enter your 10-character Permanent Account Number and click Search.
  5. The system instantly displays your application number, shares applied, shares allotted, and refund/lien status.

2. KFin Technologies Ltd (KFintech)

  1. Visit the KFintech IPO Status portal: https://ipostatus.kfintech.com/
  2. Choose any active server link (Server 1 to Server 5).
  3. Select the IPO name from the list.
  4. Select PAN, enter your PAN number, complete the security captcha, and click Submit.

3. Bigshare Services Pvt Ltd

  1. Visit the Bigshare Allotment portal: https://www.bigshareonline.com/ipo_Allotment.html
  2. Select the company from the active list.
  3. Select PAN Number, input your PAN string, enter the captcha, and click Search.

4. Cameo Corporate Services Limited

  1. Visit Cameo Corporate IPO portal: https://ipo.cameoindia.com/
  2. Select the issue name from the dropdown.
  3. Input your PAN Number or DP ID/Client ID.
  4. Click Submit to view allotment records.

5. Maashitla Securities Pvt Ltd

  1. Visit Maashitla allotment portal: https://maashitla.com/allotment-status/public-issues
  2. Select the active IPO issue.
  3. Provide your PAN or Application reference.
  4. Click Check Status.

Method 2: Checking on the BSE Allotment Verification Portal

  1. Visit the official BSE India verification page: https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli_check.aspx
  2. Select Issue Type: Equity.
  3. Select the company name from the dropdown.
  4. Enter your Application Number or your PAN Number.
  5. Solve the reCAPTCHA checkbox and click Search to view your allotment details.

Method 3: Checking on the NSE Allotment Verification Portal

  1. Visit the NSE India Public Issue verification portal: https://www.nseindia.com/products/content/equities/ipos/ipo_login.htm
  2. Log in with your free registered NSE investor credentials (or register with your PAN).
  3. Select the IPO symbol from the issue dropdown.
  4. Enter your PAN number and click Verify Bids to inspect exchange confirmation.

How to Confirm Allotment via Bank Debit Alerts

Often, the fastest way to verify allotment is by monitoring your bank account notifications on the evening of T+1 day:

  • Shares Allotted: You receive an official SMS from your bank: "Your a/c has been debited by Rs. [Amount] towards IPO allotment of [Company]..."
  • Not Allotted: You receive an SMS confirming that the UPI ASBA mandate lien has been revoked / unblocked, and your full balance is available for withdrawal.

What to Do if Your Funds Remain Blocked Past the Mandate Date?

Occasionally, due to banking clearing gateway desynchronization, your funds may remain blocked after allotment concludes. Take these steps:

  • Step 1: Check Mandate End Date: When you authorized the UPI mandate, an 'End Date' was specified (usually 10–14 days). Banks automatically drop the lien once the mandate expiry timestamp is reached.
  • Step 2: Email the Registrar with Screenshot: Send an email to the registrar's investor grievance desk (e.g. ipo.helpdesk@linkintime.co.in or einward.ris@kfintech.com) attaching your PAN and bank statement.
  • Step 3: Contact Bank Nodal Officer: Call your bank's ASBA customer care or visit the home branch to request a manual revocation of the lien.
  • Step 4: Lodge a SEBI SCORES Complaint: If unresolved within 7 days, submit a formal grievance on SEBI's SCORES 2.0 portal (scores.sebi.gov.in). Under SEBI rules, registrars and banks are liable to pay compensation at 15% per annum for delayed unblocks.

Understanding Registrar Terminology on Allotment Screen

When you query your PAN on Link Intime, KFintech, or Bigshare Services, you will encounter specific terms. Here is what they signify:

  • Shares Applied: The exact quantity of equity shares you bid for (e.g., 38 shares or 1,200 shares).
  • Shares Allotted: The quantity of shares successfully allocated to your Demat account. If this displays '0' or 'Non-Allottee', you were not picked in the lottery. If it displays the minimum lot (e.g., 38), congratulations, you won the allotment!
  • Refund Amount: In ASBA bidding, no physical refund is mailed. This field indicates the exact rupee amount that will be unblocked (lien released) by your bank.
  • Securities Transferred: Shows whether the shares have been credited electronically to your NSDL or CDSL Demat account (typically completed on T+2 day).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When is the IPO allotment status declared online?

Under SEBI's T+3 framework, allotment is typically finalized on T+1 day (the business day following the issue close date). Registrars usually publish the searchable database on their portal between late evening (8:00 PM to 11:30 PM IST) on T+1 day.

Why does my bank still show funds blocked after allotment status is declared?

Banks process mandate debits and unblocks in clearing batches through NPCI. If you were not allotted shares, the lien is normally released within 24 to 48 hours. If the lien persists past the mandate end date, you can submit an unblocking request through your net banking portal or contact your bank's ASBA nodal officer.

Can I check my allotment status without my application number?

Yes. All major registrar portals (Link Intime, KFintech, Bigshare, Cameo) as well as the BSE India portal allow you to check your allotment status using your 10-digit Permanent Account Number (PAN) alone.
