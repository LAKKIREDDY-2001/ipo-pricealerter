How to Check IPO Allotment Status Online: Step-by-Step Guide

By IPO Pricealerter Research Team Published: 04 November 2024 Updated: 15 November 2024 Reading Time: 7 mins

Once the bidding window for an Initial Public Offering closes at 5:00 PM on Day 3, investors eagerly await the Basis of Allotment finalization date. Under SEBI's modern T+3 listing framework, the entire allotment process is completed within one to two working days following the issue closure.

Whether you applied through your banking ASBA net banking facility or via discount brokers using UPI, there are multiple official, secure channels to verify your allotment outcome. In this guide, we outline the exact step-by-step methods to check your allotment status across registrars, stock exchanges, and depository accounts.

Method 1: Checking on the Designated Registrar's Portal

Every IPO has an officially appointed Registrar to the Issue responsible for validating bids, cross-referencing PAN records with CDSL/NSDL depositories, conducting computerized lottery allocations, and directing fund debits or mandate revocations.

The two largest registrar agencies in India handle over 90% of public issues:

A. Link Intime India Pvt Ltd

  1. Navigate to the official Link Intime Public Issues portal: linkintime.co.in/initial_offer/public-issues.html.
  2. In the "Select Company" dropdown, choose the name of the IPO. (Note: The company name only appears once the registrar finalizes the allotment file, typically late evening on the scheduled allotment date).
  3. Select your search parameter: PAN, Application Number, or DP Client ID. (PAN is recommended for speed).
  4. Input your 10-character alphanumeric PAN and click "Submit".
  5. The screen displays your applied shares, allotted shares, and refund status.

B. KFin Technologies Limited (KFintech)

  1. Visit KFintech's IPO Status Portal: kosmic.kfintech.com/ipostatus/.
  2. Choose from Link 1, Link 2, or Link 3 (KFintech provides mirror servers to balance heavy traffic surges).
  3. Select the IPO from the dropdown menu.
  4. Select search mode: Application No., Demat Account (DP ID & Client ID), or PAN.
  5. Enter the Captcha verification code and submit.

C. Bigshare Services Pvt Ltd (Frequently for SME IPOs)

  1. Go to Bigshare's allotment page: bigshareonline.com/ipo_allotment.html.
  2. Pick the company from the dropdown menu.
  3. Enter your PAN number or Application Number and click Search.

Method 2: Checking on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Website

The BSE provides a unified allotment status portal that aggregates data across all registrars for mainboard issues.

  1. Visit the BSE Allotment Page: bseindia.com/investors/appli_check.aspx.
  2. Under "Issue Type", select "Equity".
  3. In the "Issue Name" dropdown list, select your IPO.
  4. Enter your Application Number (which you received in your broker confirmation email) OR enter your PAN number.
  5. Check the "I'm not a robot" reCAPTCHA box and click Search.

Method 3: Checking on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) Website

The NSE offers an investor verification tool for active and recent bids:

  1. Visit nseindia.com/invest/check-trades-bids-verify-ipo-bids.
  2. You will need a registered one-time login credentials on the NSE Investor portal.
  3. Once logged in, submit your PAN to view your bidding history and allotment status.

Method 4: Bank Mandate SMS & Debit Notifications (The Fastest Signal)

Often, before registrars update their websites, your bank will process the UPI ASBA mandate instructions:

  • Successful Allotment: You receive an SMS from your bank: "Rs. 14,820 debited from account ending in XXXX towards IPO application reference XXXX." This confirms full or partial allotment.
  • Non-Allotment: You receive an SMS: "UPI mandate of Rs. 14,820 has been revoked/unblocked successfully." Your blocked bank balance is restored to your available funds.

💡 Why Has My Money Not Been Unblocked Yet?

Banks usually process mandate revocations within 24 to 48 hours following the finalization of allotment. If your bank has not unblocked your funds by the designated listing date, contact your bank's UPI support desk or lodge a complaint referencing your Application No. with the Registrar.

Method 5: Demat Account Holdings (CDSL & NSDL)

One day before the official listing date (Listing - 1 day), shares are formally credited into allotted investors' depository participant (DP) accounts.

  • Check your demat broker app (Zerodha Kite, Groww, Angel One, Upstox) under the Holdings tab. Allotted shares will appear with an interim ISIN number or company name.
  • You will also receive an automated SMS and email notification directly from CDSL or NSDL confirming the credit of equity shares.

Summary Comparison of Allotment Verification Methods

Method Availability Timing Information Required Speed & Reliability
Bank Debit/Revoke SMS Evening of Allotment Day Registered Bank Phone ⚡ Fastest indicator
Registrar Portal (Link Intime/KFin) Late Evening of Allotment Day PAN or Application Number 🎯 Detailed share count breakdown
BSE Website Late Evening / Next Morning PAN + Application Number 🔒 Official exchange record
Demat Credit (CDSL/NSDL) Day before listing (by 9 PM) Broker Login / DP SMS ✅ 100% confirmation of tradeable shares
