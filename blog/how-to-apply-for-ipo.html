How to Apply for an IPO in India — Step by Step (UPI & ASBA Guide)

By IPO PriceAlerter Research • 9 min read • Updated: November 2024

Applying for an Initial Public Offering in India was once an arduous paper-based chore involving physical share application forms, demand drafts, and weeks of postal waiting. Today, thanks to the pioneering integration of ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount) and UPI 2.0 (Unified Payments Interface) mandated by SEBI, any Indian retail investor can submit an IPO bid in less than 90 seconds directly from their smartphone.

In this comprehensive guide, we walk you through the exact, error-proof process of bidding for Mainboard and SME IPOs using your existing stock broker (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One) or Net Banking portals, ensuring you never face technical mandate rejections.

Prerequisites Before You Can Apply

Before initiating an application, ensure you possess the following three foundational accounts, all registered under the exact same Permanent Account Number (PAN):

  • Active Demat & Trading Account: Held with any SEBI-registered Depository Participant (DP) registered under NSDL or CDSL (e.g., Zerodha, Groww, Upstox).
  • Active Bank Account: Supporting ASBA / UPI mandate features with sufficient unencumbered balance matching the minimum lot size requirement.
  • UPI ID Linked to Your Bank: Registered on supported applications like Google Pay, BHIM, PhonePe, or your bank's native mobile app.
CRITICAL: Third-Party Applications are Rejected!

Under SEBI regulations, the PAN associated with your Demat account, your bank account, and your UPI ID must match identically. Applying from a family member's UPI handle or a corporate bank account for a personal Demat account leads to immediate, automated rejection at the registrar reconciliation phase.

Method 1: Applying via Stock Broker App with UPI ASBA

This is the fastest, most popular method among Indian retail investors. Follow these 5 distinct steps:

Step 1
Navigate to the IPO Section in Your Broker App

Log in to your broker app (e.g., Zerodha Kite, Groww, Upstox, Angel One) during active market hours (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST on open bidding days). Tap on the Bids or IPO tab from the navigation menu to view active issues.

Step 2
Select the Company & Enter Bid Quantities

Find the active public issue you wish to bid for and click Apply. Enter the quantity in multiples of the prescribed Lot Size (e.g., if lot size is 38 shares, entering "1 lot" auto-fills 38 shares).

Step 3
Always Tick the "Cut-off Price" Box

For book-built issues with a price band (e.g., ₹371 to ₹390), always check the Cut-off Price checkbox. This ensures your application automatically bids at the final issue price determined by the company, preventing your bid from being invalidated if the issue prices at the ceiling.

Step 4
Enter Your Personal UPI ID & Submit Bid

Carefully enter your personal VPA handle (e.g., username@okhdfcbank or username@apl). Review the total investment amount and click Submit. Your broker immediately relays the bid data to the exchange order book.

Step 5
Accept the UPI Mandate Request on Your Phone

Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM). You will receive an incoming mandate request notification from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). Review the amount, enter your secret UPI PIN, and authorize the mandate. The required funds are now blocked in your account, earning interest, until allotment day.

Method 2: Applying via Net Banking ASBA (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis)

For applications exceeding ₹5 Lakhs, High Net-worth (HNI) bids, or for investors without a UPI handle, Net Banking ASBA is the preferred institutional standard:

  1. Log in to your personal Net Banking portal (e.g., HDFC NetBanking, SBI YONO, ICICI Net Banking).
  2. Navigate to Investments & Insurance → e-Services → ASBA / IPO.
  3. Select the IPO from the list of active public issues and select your investor category (Retail or HNI).
  4. Select your Depository (NSDL or CDSL), input your 8-digit DP ID and 8-digit Client ID (BOID), along with the quantity and price.
  5. Confirm the application. Your bank blocks the funds immediately on the spot without needing an external UPI prompt.

Common Errors That Cause IPO Application Rejections

  • Mismatched PAN (Third-Party Mandate): The most common error. The primary bank account from which the mandate is authorized must belong to the Demat account holder.
  • Mandate Not Approved Before Cutoff: Submitting a bid at 4:55 PM on the final day often leads to delayed UPI notifications. If you fail to approve the mandate before 5:00 PM on closing day, your bid is canceled.
  • Multiple Bids in Same PAN in Retail Quota: Submitting multiple retail bids under the same PAN through different brokers (e.g., one on Zerodha and one on Groww) triggers an automated SEBI compliance duplicate flag, canceling all your applications.
  • Insufficient Balance in Linked Account: Ensure that your bank balance strictly exceeds the total lot value before approving the mandate.
