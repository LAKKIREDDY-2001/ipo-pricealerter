Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2024 • Last Revised: November 15, 2024

At IPO Pricealerter (accessible from https://pricealerter.in/, hereinafter referred to as "the Website", "we", "our", or "us"), the privacy and confidential integrity of our visitors' personal information is of paramount importance to us. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document details the types of personal and non-personal data collected and recorded by IPO Pricealerter, how we store and utilize such information, and the rights available to users regarding their online privacy.

This Privacy Policy applies solely to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in IPO Pricealerter. This policy does not apply to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website.

1. Consent & Acceptance

By accessing or using our website, you hereby unequivocally consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to all terms, stipulations, and disclosures outlined herein. If you do not agree with any provision of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue your access and use of our platform immediately.

2. Information We Collect

IPO Pricealerter is committed to the principle of data minimization. The personal information that you are requested to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to submit your data:

  • Direct Communications: If you contact our research desk, editorial team, or grievance officer directly via email (e.g., at contact@pricealerter.in or lakkistudy@gmail.com), we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of your message, attachments you may provide, and any other information you choose to supply.
  • Interactive Comments & Community Input: If you contribute to community sentiment polls or IPO comment sections, your self-chosen nickname and posted comments are processed. We do not store passwords, bank account numbers, or demat credentials.
  • Newsletter & Alert Subscriptions: If you opt-in to receive email updates regarding newly filed IPO prospectuses or Grey Market Premium (GMP) alerts, we collect and store your valid email address solely for delivering tersebut communications.

3. Standard Log Files

IPO Pricealerter follows a standard operational procedure of utilizing server log files. These files log visitors when they access web pages across the site. All leading web hosting corporations and content delivery networks (CDNs) maintain this practice as a component of hosting service analytics.

The non-personally identifiable information collected by our log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where feasible)
  • Browser user-agent, operating system, and hardware architecture
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps of request cycles
  • Referring pages, exit URLs, and navigation flow through the site
  • Aggregate click tallies and bounce telemetry

The fundamental objective of this diagnostic data is to analyze macroeconomic traffic trends, administer the technical stability of the website, prevent distributed denial of service (DDoS) attempts, track aggregate audience movement across IPO pages, and assemble broad demographic metrics for performance optimization. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

8. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers are granted specific statutory rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: The right to request disclosure of the specific categories and pieces of personal data that a business has collected about them.
  • Right to Deletion: The right to request the deletion of any personal data collected by the business.
  • Right to Opt-Out: The right to direct a business that sells or shares consumer data not to sell or share their personal information. IPO Pricealerter does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties for monetary consideration.

If you make a CCPA request, we have one month to respond to you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our team at privacy@pricealerter.in.

9. GDPR Data Protection Rights

For users residing within the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we adhere strictly to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to the following rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

Requests submitted under GDPR will receive a formal response within 30 business days.
