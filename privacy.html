Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how PriceAlerter ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit pricealerter.in, use its IPO and listed-share tools, or request email alerts. We aim to collect only information needed to operate and improve the website.

Privacy contact: lakkistudy@gmail.com. PriceAlerter is an independently operated information website, not a bank, broker or investment adviser.

1. Information you provide

  • Email alert requests: If you enter an email address to request IPO updates, the address and subscription source are sent to the website's subscription endpoint. If that endpoint is unavailable, the site may use FormSubmit's email-processing service to forward the request to the site operator. The browser may also keep a local copy of subscription details for the website interface.
  • Contact messages: If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you include in your message or attachments.
  • Browser preferences: The website may use browser local storage to remember interface preferences, selected filters or privacy-notice dismissal. This information remains in that browser unless cleared.

Do not submit passwords, UPI PINs, bank or demat credentials, PAN, Aadhaar, payment-card details or other sensitive identity information through the website or email.

2. Analytics and technical information

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage and improve performance. Analytics technologies may process page URLs, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser information, referral source and interaction events. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Our hosting and security providers may also process technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, requested page and request time, to deliver and protect the website. We do not control their infrastructure-level log retention.

3. Advertising cookies and third parties

PriceAlerter uses Google AdSense advertising services. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve and measure ads and help prevent invalid activity. Depending on your location and advertising settings, these technologies may be used for personalised or non-personalised advertising. Third parties may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons or IP addresses, as a result of ad serving on this website.

To learn how Google uses data on partner websites, visit Google's partner-sites disclosure. You can manage Google advertising personalisation at Google Ads Settings. Browser settings can also be used to restrict or delete cookies. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or advertising. Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies; we do not control their processing practices.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to operate requested features, deliver email alert requests, respond to messages, maintain security, prevent abuse, understand aggregate usage and improve the website. We do not sell personal information to data brokers. We do not use email alert addresses to send unrelated marketing unless you separately agree to it.

5. Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for its purpose, to maintain service records or to meet applicable legal requirements. Browser-local information can be cleared through browser settings. To ask about an email address submitted for alerts or request correction or deletion, contact lakkistudy@gmail.com from the address concerned and describe your request. Some copies may remain in backups or third-party systems for their own retention periods.

6. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information so we can review the request.

7. Your choices

  • You can choose not to submit an email address or contact details.
  • You can clear browser storage and cookies using browser settings.
  • You can manage Google ad personalisation through Google Ads Settings.
  • You can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The revision date at the top indicates the latest version.

9. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email lakkistudy@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, financial credentials or identity documents in your message.
