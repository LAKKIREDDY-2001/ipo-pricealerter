How to Check IPO Allotment Status in 3 Simple Steps

Step 1: Open Official Registrar

Check which registrar was assigned in the IPO red herring prospectus (Link Intime, KFintech, Bigshare, etc.) and open their official portal.

Step 2: Select Company & Mode

Select the IPO name from the dropdown. Choose identification mode: PAN Card Number (fastest), Application Number, or Demat DP/Client ID.

Step 3: View Allotment & Refund

Enter captcha and click Submit. You will see total shares applied, shares allotted (0 if not allotted), and refund mandate unblock status.
